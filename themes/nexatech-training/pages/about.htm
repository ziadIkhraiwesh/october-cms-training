About NexaTech

Learning CMS Development Through Practice

This project was created during the Blue Full-Stack Training Program to explore how October CMS organizes reusable and manageable websites.

Our Approach

We separate the shared website structure from individual page content. The main layout controls the document structure, while partials provide reusable sections such as the header, footer, and hero.

Task 20 Focus

  • Custom October CMS theme
  • Reusable layouts and partials
  • CMS-managed content
  • Responsive public pages
